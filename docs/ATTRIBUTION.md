# Attribution and data

CrossCue was built on October 30, 2026 for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon. The related MIT-licensed [Second Feature](https://github.com/himanshu748/second-feature) repository, revision 3c3fea36fdba2ddfbfcad626d00f0e66864fe28d, was inspected for existing Qloo adapter patterns. Its film-pairing product and repository were preserved. CrossCue's typed adapter, finite scheduler, consequence policy, state workflow and interface are separate implementation work.

Qloo identities and directional rankings come from the event API, with query provenance and original timestamps. Qloo names/IDs and raw contextual scores are provider data, not covered by a claim that this project's MIT license relicenses Qloo's underlying database. Retain only the small fixed festival evidence needed for reproducibility. No bulk database reconstruction, personal data or demographic inference.

Artist and film names identify public cultural works. This fictional timetable does not imply artist participation, endorsement, venue booking or screening rights. Film blocks include illustrative discussion/reset time and are scheduling durations, not asserted exact runtimes. No external poster art or music is required by the interface.

Dependencies retain their respective licenses. No generated narration or video is included.
